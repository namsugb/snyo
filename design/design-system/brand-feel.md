# Brand Feel

## Project Goal

This project is a wedding invitation website. It should feel like a personal invitation from a couple, not like a generic event landing page.

## Emotional Direction

The experience should feel:

- Warm
- Romantic
- Personal
- Soft
- Handcrafted
- Nostalgic
- Light and joyful

## Visual Impression

- The page should feel mobile-first and intimate, as if someone is opening a carefully made digital letter.
- The overall mood should borrow from printed keepsakes, handwritten notes, and polaroid photo albums.
- The design should feel charming and memorable, while still being readable and easy to navigate.
- Decorative details are welcome when they support the invitation mood.

## Reference Interpretation

- `design/reference/gallery.jpg` suggests a polaroid-like photo gallery that feels tactile and scrapbook-inspired.
- `design/reference/mood1.jpg` and `design/reference/mood2.jpg` suggest soft, sentimental styling with handwritten accents and a romantic presentation.
- `design/reference/movingimage1.png` and `design/reference/movingimage2.png` suggest a subtle frame-swapping effect that makes text or illustrated elements feel gently animated.

## What To Emphasize

- A strong first impression centered on the couple and the wedding date
- Emotional storytelling through greeting copy and photography
- A soft handmade tone through handwriting-style accents, layered image framing, and gentle motion
- A layout that feels beautiful on phones first, then expands gracefully on larger screens

## What To Avoid

- Corporate SaaS-style layouts
- Harsh geometric minimalism with no warmth
- Dense interface chrome or dashboard-like sections
- Overly flashy animation or loud color contrast
- Generic stock-wedding aesthetics with no personality
