# Colors

## Direction

Use a soft wedding palette that feels warm, airy, and romantic. The page should feel like paper, flowers, photos, and handwritten ink rather than a high-contrast product UI.

## Suggested Roles

- Background: the main page tone, similar to soft paper or warm fabric
- Surface: cards, message blocks, gallery frames, and upload panels
- Text Primary: main readable text for names and content
- Text Secondary: supporting descriptive text
- Accent Rose: romantic highlights and small key details
- Accent Sage: calm natural accent for balancing warm tones
- Border: subtle separators and photo-frame edges
- Ink Accent: a darker handwritten or decorative emphasis tone

## Usage Rules

- Keep the palette soft and avoid harsh black-and-white contrast unless needed for readability.
- Use rose and sage as restrained accents, not as dominant full-screen color blocks.
- Maintain good contrast for important wedding information such as names, date, and venue.
- Photo frames, paper cards, and handwritten labels can use slightly warmer tones than the page background.
- Avoid neon, overly saturated primaries, or cold corporate blues.
